Data, method and limitations

How these figures were prepared

Source: the course-provided Australian television energy-rating CSV, tv_2026_09_22.csv, a snapshot dated 22 September 2026. It contains registered television models, product availability, display technology, screen size, average-mode power and energy-label fields.

  • Start with 5,036 CSV rows and keep the highest numeric Submit_ID for each Model_No: 4,771 models remain.
  • Keep records marked Available (4,768), then retain models whose SoldIn field includes Australia (4,591).
  • Round screen diagonals from centimetres to the nearest inch for the size chart. Brand capitalization is consolidated while distinct registered brand names remain separate.
  • Power summaries use median Avg_mode_power. The star-rating plot uses Star2, the displayed rating field. Correlations use records with both required numeric values.

The snapshot changes as products are registered or removed. Model counts show listed products, not sales or market share. Rated power comes from product test information; the dataset has no price, household viewing hours or measured household bills. Technology comparisons are not adjusted for screen size. Brand variants can be counted separately.

The dataset contains product and registration details, with no personal household information. The download retains its original fields for transparency.
